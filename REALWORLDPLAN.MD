# REALWORLDPLAN — from virtual community to real-world gathering

Goal: LivingBook shouldn't stop at 1-on-1 chats with a book. Books have always been
social objects — clubs, study groups, signings, pilgrimages. This plan sequences in-app
features that first bring users together **virtually around a book's agent**, then use
that gravity to produce **physical, real-world interaction**, in the spirit of Pokémon
gyms: persistent places, friendly rivalry, and a reason to show up in person.

## Phase 1 — Virtual togetherness (in-app, launch window)

1. **Reading Rooms.** Every book gets a live group room where the book's agent is the
   host. Members ask questions publicly; the agent answers once, on video, for the whole
   room (one Masky conversation, everyone watching the same live URL). Scheduled
   "office hours" sessions — e.g. the Bible narrator answers live every evening.
2. **Favorites → Circles.** Favoriting a book joins its Circle: a lightweight feed of
   the best recent agent answers, member shares, and author drops (new podcast uploaded,
   new Q&A transcript). Members can up-vote answers; top answers become the book's
   public highlight reel.
3. **Knowledge Trials.** Quiz ladders generated strictly from the book's knowledge base
   (verbatim-sourced questions). Rank titles per book — Novice → Scholar → **Keeper**.
   This is the gym-badge mechanic: rank is per-book and per-region, so there can be a
   "Keeper of Genesis, Austin TX".
4. **Duets & Debates.** Two-avatar conversations (Masky supports scripting both sides):
   users stage a debate between two books' agents — or between themselves (their Masky
   avatar) and the book — and share the clip. Social-first, made to be reshared.
5. **Author AMAs.** Authors schedule live sessions where their own avatar co-hosts with
   the book's agent; fan questions the author answers live get ingested (with consent)
   as new transcripts — the agent literally gets smarter from every gathering.

## Phase 2 — Bridging online → offline

6. **Local Chapters.** Circles gain geography: opt-in city-level chapters with their own
   feed and leaderboard. The app surfaces "12 members of the Dune Circle within 10 miles".
7. **Story Landmarks (the gyms).** Real-world places tied to books: the church downtown
   (Bible), the library, the indie bookstore, a setting from the novel, an author's
   hometown. Landmarks appear on an in-app map. Checking in at a landmark unlocks
   location-specific agent content — the narrator reads the passage that happened
   "here", or the local Keeper's favorite verse. First-visit bonuses, streaks for
   weekly visits.
8. **Keeper challenges.** The current Keeper of a book's local chapter "holds" its
   landmark. Others can challenge via a Knowledge Trial taken **at the landmark**
   (geofenced). Win → you're the new Keeper, your avatar is displayed on the landmark
   for everyone. Friendly, Pokémon-gym-style turnover.

## Phase 3 — Real-world events

9. **Meetup engine.** Any chapter member above a rank threshold can propose a meetup at
   a Story Landmark (RSVP in-app). The book's agent generates the session kit: discussion
   questions from real fan Q&A history, a themed quiz, and a welcome video addressed to
   the attendees by name.
10. **Live Landmark sessions.** At the meetup, the book's agent runs on a shared screen
    (the Masky live URL in any browser/OBS) as the event's moderator — reading passages
    on request, judging the quiz, recording a group "we were here" clip everyone can
    reshare. Attendance stamps a verifiable badge.
11. **Author tours & bookstore partnerships.** Authors see which chapters are most
    active and route signings/readings there; bookstores and churches host landmark
    events and get foot traffic + in-app promotion. This is also a revenue surface:
    sponsored landmarks, ticketed author events, credit-earning for hosts.
12. **Pilgrimages.** Multi-landmark quests: visit the five Story Landmarks of a book in
    one season to earn a rare badge and a personalized thank-you video from the author's
    avatar. For the Bible: a city's "seven churches" trail as the flagship example.

## Design principles

- **The agent is the campfire.** Every physical feature centers on the book's agent
  doing something live that solo readers can't get: addressing the group, judging, and
  memorializing the gathering as shareable video.
- **Verbatim integrity everywhere.** Quizzes, session kits, and location content are
  generated only from the book's knowledge base — the real-world layer inherits the
  same no-invention rule as chat.
- **Consent + safety first.** Geography is opt-in per Circle, coarse by default
  (city-level), precise location only for active landmark check-ins; minors excluded
  from location features; meetups only at public venues.
- **Self-evolving loop.** Every AMA, meetup Q&A, and challenge feeds new transcripts
  into the knowledge base — physical gatherings are literally how the agent grows.
